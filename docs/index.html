3‑DOF Pitch · Roll · Yaw

Battery power · Wheelchair motor lever arms · Drift effect

🔋 No PSU – 24V Battery Bank

Two 12V batteries in series · regen absorbed · should need charge ~2 weeks.

Module 1 & 2 : Pitch / Roll

  • Left + right independent lever arms
  • 70mm crank → M8 truss rod → platform
  • Worm‑drive motors hold position
  • ~240mm rod end centre distance
images/pitch-roll-lever.jpg

Module 3 : Yaw / Traction Loss

  • Central motor + Yaw Wheel
  • 70mm crank → lateral carriage
  • Drift effect / side‑sway
  • Stroke ~120mm
images/20221208_120139.jpg

Electronics & Control

  • ESP32‑S3 with 1kHz PID
  • AS5600 encoders on motor shafts
  • BTS7960 drivers (3x)

Through‑Frame Pivots

M8 threaded rod passes completely through the wood with washers and nyloc nuts (that's a long nut). Distributes force, prevents tear‑out.

Battery Safety

40A fuse on positive, isolator switch accessible from seat. Batteries strapped down in base torsion box.
